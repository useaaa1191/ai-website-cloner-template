# ContentSection Specification

## Overview
- **Target file:** `src/components/ContentSection.tsx`
- **Screenshots:** `block-02` through `block-05`
- **Interaction model:** hover (opacity on title)

## Variants
- **news:** 2-col grid of horizontal 120px thumb + title/meta rows
- **stories / research / business:** 3-col square image cards

## Styles
- Section heading: ~28–32px / 600
- View more/all: 14px link, right-aligned
- Card title: 17–18px / 500
- Meta: 13px / muted rgba(0,0,0,0.55)
- Image radius: ~12–16px (news), ~16px (cards)
- Gap: 24px

## Content
Recent news (6), Stories (3), Latest research (3), OpenAI for business (3)
